Booking {{ version }}

The calendar for the lab's rooms and equipment: meeting rooms, microscopes, growth chambers, whatever needs sharing. Booked ahead on a week calendar or by finding a free slot, with the people in each booking, and shown in the Lab Notebook on the day. How to use it →

What it holds

A week calendar

Each room or instrument on its own calendar, today marked, bookings in the colour of the thing booked. Move a week at a time or jump to a date.

Find a slot

Say what kind of thing you need, for how long, roughly when and where: it lists the free slots rather than making you read every calendar.

People in a booking

Tag whoever is taking part. It then shows in their bookings and on their notebook day, so a shared session is on everyone's calendar.

Rooms and equipment

Added by a lab's admins: what it is, where it is, how many it seats, and who may book it.

Each thing has its own rules

Whoever adds a room or instrument sets how it is booked, and the calendar then keeps to it:

  • the steps it is booked in — a microscope by the half hour, a growth chamber by the day;
  • the shortest and longest a booking may be;
  • how far ahead it may be booked, so nobody takes the confocal for all of next term;
  • the days and hours it is open.

Its managers are not held to the limits on how far ahead, so a course or a service visit can be put in the calendar early.

Who sees and books what

  • A room or instrument is shared with the labs and chair groups its managers choose, or with everyone in the suite.
  • Anyone it is shared with can see its calendar and book it.
  • Its managers — the lab admins of the lab that added it — change its rules, and can move or cancel anyone's booking.
  • You can always change or cancel your own bookings.

With the other tools

A day page in the Lab Notebook shows what you booked that day, with the room or instrument and the time. That way the notebook knows which microscope a picture came from without anyone writing it down, and a booking you were tagged in appears on your day as well.

Where the data is kept

On the lab's own server, in its own database, backed up each night with the rest of the suite. Times are kept in UTC and shown in the lab's own time zone, so a booking stays put when the clocks change.
